About Me

Focused on performance, visibility, and growth

I work with small businesses, local companies, and startups to strengthen how their websites perform and how easily they're found online. My focus spans technical SEO, on-page and off-page optimization, local search visibility, and WordPress website development — always with an emphasis on clean structure, measurable improvements, and a better experience for real visitors.

Whether it's auditing a site's technical health, researching the right keywords to target, or building a fast and well-structured WordPress site from the ground up, my goal is the same: give businesses a digital presence that supports real growth, not just surface-level metrics.
